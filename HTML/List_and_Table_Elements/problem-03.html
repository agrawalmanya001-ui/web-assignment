Practical Class Preparation

Students should follow these steps before attending a practical class:

  1. Read the practical instructions carefully.
  2. Revise the required theoretical concepts.
  3. Bring the practical notebook and required materials.
  4. Complete the previous practical assignment.
  5. Follow all laboratory safety and discipline rules.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A classroom session where theoretical concepts are explained by the faculty.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or write programs.
Tutorial
A session focused on practice, problem solving and doubt clarification.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments and activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity used to develop practical skills.
