Students Lead a New Wave of Environmental Awareness

Young Minds Take Action to Build a Cleaner Future

Students across the city have come together to promote environmental awareness and encourage people to adopt cleaner and more sustainable habits. The campaign focuses on reducing waste, saving water, planting trees, and keeping public places clean. More than 500 students participated in the campaign.

The main event was organised at City Central Park on 24 August 2026. Students participated in tree plantation activities, cleanliness drives, and awareness programmes. They also spoke to local residents about the importance of protecting the environment and reducing plastic waste.

Organisers said that the campaign is not only about cleaning the surroundings but also about creating long-term awareness among young people. Small actions taken every day can create a big difference in the future. Students are planning to continue the campaign through regular cleanliness drives and educational activities.

Editor's Note

Environmental protection is everyone's responsibility. Young people can play an important role by spreading awareness and encouraging positive changes in their schools, homes, and communities.

School Hosts Annual Technology Exhibition

Students Showcase Innovative Projects and Creative Ideas

A technology exhibition was organised at a local school where students presented creative projects based on science, technology, and innovation. The exhibition gave students an opportunity to demonstrate their ideas and explain how technology can be used to solve everyday problems. More than 100 student projects were displayed.

The exhibition was held on 24 August 2026 at the School Auditorium. Projects included smart devices, environmental solutions, educational models, and basic artificial intelligence applications. Teachers and parents visited the exhibition and appreciated the efforts of the students.

The organisers encouraged students to continue exploring new ideas and developing practical skills. Innovation begins with curiosity and the courage to try something new. The event also helped students improve their communication, teamwork, and presentation skills.

Editor's Note

Technology can become a powerful tool when it is used creatively and responsibly. School exhibitions like this encourage students to think differently, learn through practical experience, and develop solutions for real-world problems.
