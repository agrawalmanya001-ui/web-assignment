Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is represented by the chemical formula H2O.

Carbon dioxide is represented by the formula CO2.

Oxygen gas is represented by O2.

Sulfuric acid has the chemical formula H2SO4.

Sodium chloride is represented by NaCl.

An atom is the smallest unit of an element that retains the properties of that element.

Chemical reactions involve the formation of new substances from existing substances.

Physics

The SI unit of area is expressed in m2.

The SI unit of volume is expressed in m3.

The speed of light is approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

The acceleration due to gravity on Earth is approximately 9.8 m/s2.

Force is a push or pull that can change the motion of an object.

Gravity is the force that attracts objects towards the Earth.

Biology

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Plants use photosynthesis to prepare their own food using sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water.

During photosynthesis, plants release oxygen and produce glucose as a food source.

The human body contains approximately 37 × 1012 cells.

DNA contains genetic information that helps determine the characteristics of living organisms.

Quick Revision

Chemistry: H2O is water, CO2 is carbon dioxide, and O2 is oxygen.

Physics: Area is measured in m2, volume in m3, and the speed of light is approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

Biology: The cell is the basic unit of life, and photosynthesis helps plants prepare their food.
