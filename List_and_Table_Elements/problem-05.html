Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026! Join us for three exciting days filled with competitions, cultural events, entertainment, learning activities and unforgettable memories.

Fest Introduction:

Dear Students,
It gives me immense pleasure to announce that the Annual Cultural Fest “SPANDAN” of our university is scheduled to take place on April 14-15, 2026. This fest is one of the most awaited events of the year and brings together students from all departments to showcase their talents and cultural diversity. The Cultural Fest will include a range of events such as singing, dancing, poetry, theater, and other performances. It will also feature various competitions and contests that will challenge your skills and creativity. The fest is an opportunity for you to express your talents and showcase your cultural roots. We invite all the students to participate in this cultural fest and make it a grand success. We encourage you to come up with innovative ideas and prepare well for the competitions. Your active participation and involvement will make this event a memorable one. We also request all the students to adhere to the guidelines and regulations set forth by the university during the event. We must maintain discipline and decorum throughout the fest to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone. We look forward to your enthusiastic participation in the Annual Cultural Fest and wish you all the very best.

Registration Process:-

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Select the event in which you want to participate.
  3. Fill in your personal and college details.
  4. Select individual or team participation.
  5. Submit the registration form.
  6. Save your registration confirmation.
  7. Report to the event venue on the scheduled date.

General Fest Rules:-

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report at the venue on time.
  • Discipline must be maintained throughout the fest.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • Any form of misconduct may lead to disqualification.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.

Event Definitions:-

Open Event:
An event that is open to all eligible students participating in the college fest.
Team Event:
A competition in which participants compete as a group or team.
Individual Event:
A competition in which one participant competes individually.
Registration:
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator:
A person responsible for managing and conducting an event.
Final Round:
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete for the final positions.
